Planner — how to install and open it

1. Drag Planner onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open Planner from Applications (or Launchpad).

The first time, macOS says it can't check Planner for malicious software
(it's a free app made by a friend, not from the App Store). To open it anyway:

   • Click "Done" on that message.
   • Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   • Scroll down to the message about Planner and click "Open Anyway".
   • Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open".

You only have to do this once.

Tips
- File > New Planner (⇧⌘N) starts a fresh planner; ⌘O shows all your planners.
- Click the paw in the top bar to pick a pet. Turn on "Roam on my desktop" to let it
  walk around your screen; right-click it to bring it back.
- In a list's ⋯ menu, "Open in its own window" pops the list out as a small floating window.
- The paw in the menu bar has the pet controls too.

Your planners are saved on your Mac in ~/Library/Application Support/Planner.
