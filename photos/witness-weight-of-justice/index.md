On May 9, 2023, Norberto Peets was exonerated after serving over 23 years for a crime he didn’t commit. Wrongly convicted of attempted murder in 1999, his case was marked by eyewitness misidentification and a defense that failed him.

These photographs document a quiet, powerful gathering where Mr. Peets shared his story — not just of injustice, but of survival. Surrounded by students, educators, and advocates, he spoke with grace and clarity about time lost and truth reclaimed.

I felt I had to document this moment. It wasn’t just about capturing a talk — it was about honoring presence, listening, and the weight of what we still owe the wrongfully convicted.
