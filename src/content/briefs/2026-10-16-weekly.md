Sources: WSJ Tech, FT Technology, FT World/Markets, The Information, and CPI coverage (CNBC, CBS, TheStreet, Yahoo Finance).

## The one-paragraph story
Oil (Iran war, Houthi strikes on Saudi Arabia, Brent ~$110) pushed September CPI to 3.7% and the 10-year Treasury yield above 5.3% (highest since 2002). Then the AI trade broke at its weakest point, the debt that funds data centers. Failed data-center IPOs (Firmus, AirTrunk), distressed CoreWeave bonds and Anthropic paying ~10% on a $60bn chip financing triggered a risk-off week. The Nasdaq 100 fell 5.5%, its worst week since the April 2025 tariff shock. Nvidia lost more than $350bn in market value and chip stocks (SOX) fell more than 11%. Treasuries rallied as a safe haven even on hot inflation (10-year ~5.08%), and money rotated into energy, financials, defensives and the Dow.

## 1. Macro: inflation, rates, Fed
- **CPI (Sep, released Oct 14):** +0.6% m/m, 3.7% y/y (from 3.4%). Core 0.3% m/m, 2.6% y/y. Both slightly above consensus. Gasoline +9.1% m/m was about half the gain, and US diesel is ~$6.31/gal.
- **Fed (Oct 27–28):** after a September hike to 3.75–4.00%, October hike odds spiked to ~35–48% on CPI day. The bond rally and Chair Warsh giving no guidance shifted expectations back toward a hold. Check CME FedWatch.
- **Global:** ECB (euro-area inflation 3.8%) and BoE (UK CPI ~3.4% expected) also face rate-hike risk.
- **Energy politics:** ahead of the midterms, Trump waived new Russia sanctions and is weighing easing curbs on the shadow fleet to cut diesel prices. The IEA sees a diesel shortage until mid-2027.

## 2. AI and tech: the financing layer is cracking
- **IPO window shutting:** Firmus pulled its IPO (~$30bn valuation), AirTrunk postponed its Singapore REIT, and SB Energy and OpenAI delayed their IPOs (FT). Nscale is still pitching a $35bn IPO after Microsoft cancelled a UK lease over grid power. DayOne (69% of revenue from ByteDance) is pushing ahead with a ≤$5B New York IPO (The Information).
- **Neocloud debt stress:** CoreWeave bonds fell below 80c (~15% yield), its 5-year CDS went above 11 points, and the stock fell ~25% on the week. Nebius also fell ~24%. Jensen Huang's vote of confidence gave only a brief bounce.
- **AI labs' cost of capital:** Anthropic's $60bn "Project North Star" TPU financing pays Blackstone ~10% on $9bn of junior debt. Anthropic is rated one notch below investment grade, and its Q3 revenue grew ~50% to $16.8bn, slower than hoped.
- **Physical limits:** at least 47 large data-center projects were delayed or blocked in 2026, and about $130bn of projects were disrupted in Q1 alone (WSJ). Bernstein thinks only ~35% of the US pipeline is credible.
- **Margins:** Microsoft's Azure chief reportedly asked for 20% cost cuts as capex drags free cash flow down ~20% (The Information; Microsoft disputes it).
- **Pricing pressure:** the top-10 AI models' share of developer usage fell from 55% to 14% in a year as cheaper "good enough" models win (The Information).
- **Lab revenue:** OpenAI's ad business reached ~$1B annualized with 3× more advertisers but is likely to miss its $2.4B target. OpenAI is also in talks to buy Trail of Bits, a move into cybersecurity.
- **Chips:** memory sold off hardest (SK Hynix ~−20%) on fears that prices have peaked and China's CXMT is catching up. Conflict to flag: Dell says the memory *shortage* will get worse next year. Both can be true (tight supply now, a downturn feared later).
- **Agents and finance:** Schwab, JPMorgan and Citi are blocking third-party AI agents from customer accounts. The BoE's Bailey calls AI agents a financial-stability issue.
- **Insurance:** AI liability coverage tops out around $50M, which is a disclosure risk for the Anthropic and OpenAI IPOs (WSJ).
- **China:** draft export controls cover top AI models and chip, memory and packaging technology. China ordered Meta's $2bn Manus deal unwound.

## 3. Global and political
- **France:** the worst riots in years, from cost-of-living marches (~2,400 arrests). Le Pen leads ahead of the election in six months. French debt worries are hitting French stocks.
- **Ukraine/Russia:** one of the largest Russian strikes on Ukraine's grid. The US is reportedly pressing Kyiv to stop hitting Russian refineries.
- **Also on the calendar:** Alberta independence referendum (Oct 19), Brazil's presidential run-off, and the US–EU dispute over Starlink spectrum.

## 5. Why it matters for IB recruiting (interview talking points)
- **ECM/DCM:** a textbook "window closing" case. Firmus failed on valuation, ~6× leverage and lock-ups. Morgan Stanley, JPM and BofA lose fees, and issuers move to pricier private credit (Anthropic at ~10%).
- **Private credit and AI:** debt-funded AI capex is the market's biggest fragility. Compare Dell (customers fund its inventory, negative cash conversion cycle) with CoreWeave (heavy debt).
- **Restructuring:** distressed neocloud debt is a possible future mandate pipeline.
- **Boutiques:** FT reports Perella Weinberg's failed sale to Piper Sandler and Evercore naming co-presidents, both relevant consolidation news.
