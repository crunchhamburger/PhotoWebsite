## Top Takeaways
- Anthropic priced its planned listing below what investors were discussing a week ago, after last week's tech slide. Several of its own chip and cloud suppliers are lined up to buy shares alongside the offering.
- US consumer prices rose faster than forecast in October, and the increase was broad rather than just energy. Markets now treat a December Fed hike as close to certain, and short-term Treasury yields jumped.
- CoreWeave's traded bonds imply that new unsecured debt for GPU cloud providers costs around 15%. Its results after Tuesday's close are the next read on that.
- New reporting ties Nvidia's chief executive to a channel that moved restricted chips into China. Export-control bills are also gaining ground in Congress.
- SoftBank booked a quarterly loss that included writing off deposits on power equipment for US data centers, a first concrete cost of reworking AI build-out plans.
- Oil fell below $90 a barrel as the US played down new strikes on Iran, while money kept moving out of tech into defensive sectors.

## Numbers Worth Remembering
- Anthropic: about $1.3T at its indicated range; revenue run rate about $81B.
- October CPI: +0.4% for the month, core +0.3%, each a tenth above forecast.
- Odds of a December Fed hike: about 90%.
- CoreWeave's 2032 bond: yielding about 15%.
- SoftBank write-off on data-center equipment deposits: $3.7B.
- Brent crude: under $90, down about a quarter in November.
- Global consumer staples: best month since 1998 in October.
