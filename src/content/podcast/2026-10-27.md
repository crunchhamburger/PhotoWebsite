## Top Takeaways
- Today's edition covers prices and markets only. The news collection didn't run this morning.
- The cost of renting a top-end AI chip is flat on the day but up about a quarter over the past month. It sits just below its mid-October peak, a sign that demand for compute is still keeping pace with supply.
- A leading memory-industry tracker raised its outlook for next year's high-bandwidth memory prices sharply. That's good news for the three companies that make it.
- Everyday memory chip prices barely moved. Newer chips are edging up and older ones edging down.
- Korean stocks jumped about 3%, led by SK Hynix, after a chip-led rally on Wall Street on Monday.
- European shares lagged, pulled down by a sharp fall in HSBC after its results.
- Oil eased on hopes for diplomacy with Iran, though US crude is still above $100.
- Wednesday brings the Fed decision and results from Microsoft and Alphabet. Their spending plans matter more for chip prices than the rate call does.
