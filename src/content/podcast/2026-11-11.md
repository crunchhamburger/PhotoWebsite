## Top Takeaways
- A second warm core inflation reading has investors treating a December Fed hike as a given. Short-term yields had their sharpest daily rise in well over a year, and the 10-year briefly went above 5%.
- CoreWeave beat revenue expectations and grew its order book. It plans to pay for its build-out with loans backed by its chips and with customer prepayments, not costly unsecured bonds.
- Anthropic's IPO may price above its filed range, with large chip and cloud partners buying shares alongside it. It also launched office tools that compete with Microsoft and Google, which are themselves its investors and cloud suppliers.
- A filing shows Anthropic shifting more compute spending to the big cloud providers. A large lease with a smaller provider can now be cancelled after a missed deadline.
- OpenAI's sales grew quickly last quarter, but its losses grew too, and its current funding round is well short of its target.
- Google's search ad growth slowed as its AI answers show fewer ads, and some advertisers are moving budget to Meta and ChatGPT.
- SoftBank gave up large turbine reservations and the fees it had paid for them. Investors are also pulling back from AI-focused nuclear and fuel-cell start-ups.
- Regulators delayed GPU rental-price futures, and a proposed export blacklisting tied to the Nvidia smuggling probe has stalled.

## Numbers Worth Remembering
- Core CPI: +0.3% for October.
- 10-year Treasury: closed just under 5% after briefly topping it.
- CoreWeave order backlog: about $133B; quarterly revenue about $3.7B.
- Anthropic IPO: valuation of $1.5T or more under discussion; proceeds could top $100B.
- OpenAI quarterly revenue: about $10B, with an adjusted operating loss of about $11B.
- Google search ad growth: 12%, down from 17%.
- SoftBank turbine reservations cancelled: more than 13 GW.
