## Top Takeaways
- The midterm results put candidates who promised to pause data-center construction into office in Texas, Georgia and Ohio, states that hold more than 40% of planned US capacity.
- Two days later US stocks lost about $3.2 trillion of value in a single session. The Nasdaq 100 fell 6.4% and chip stocks had their second-worst day since 1994.
- Forced selling by hedge funds crowded into the same AI trades made the drop far bigger than the policy news alone.
- Anthropic still plans to go public before Thanksgiving, but investors now expect a valuation of roughly $1.25–1.75 trillion rather than $2 trillion.
- Current AI demand held up: AMD's data-center sales nearly doubled and GPU rental prices touched a three-month high. The market is marking down future capacity, not today's spending.
- Nvidia's board opened an independent inquiry after reports that its chief executive was the target of a foreign intelligence effort.
- October payrolls beat forecasts by a wide margin, keeping a December Fed rate hike likely, while oil fell about 13% on hopes for an Iran deal.
