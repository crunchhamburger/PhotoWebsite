## Top Takeaways
- SK Hynix earned a record operating profit of about $62.5bn last quarter, with margins near 80%. It says its memory output is spoken for through 2029, and Korea's main index closed above 6,000 for the first time.
- The rental price of a top Nvidia AI chip rose about 13% in one day to a three-month high. Demand for computing power is outrunning new supply.
- Anthropic's IPO filing, showing revenue up roughly fourteen-fold in the first half, lifted chip stocks. Broadcom gained about 4% after it agreed to back a $63B chip-leasing vehicle for Anthropic, which also exposes it to credit risk.
- OpenAI plans to buy Google's AI chips, giving Nvidia's biggest customer a second supplier.
- Big US banks are lending more for data centers but passing much of the risk on to bond investors.
- Roughly $300B of risky private-equity loans come due in 2028–29, a third of it in software. Bank of America expects defaults to double next year.
- Onsemi is negotiating a ~$1B deal for a chip-power startup, and TPG a ~$5bn deal for UK data-center operator Ark.
- China's state funds are buying stocks to stop a slide, but weak spending limits the upside.
- The Fed decides today, with a hold expected. Microsoft and Alphabet report after the close.

## Numbers Worth Remembering
