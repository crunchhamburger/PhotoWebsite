## Top Takeaways
- Nvidia's board held urgent talks on whether Jensen Huang should step aside after a second personal relationship was reported. No decision had been announced by early Thursday, the company has no published succession plan, and the stock fell sharply after hours and before the open.
- Companies that lean on Nvidia's financing, including CoreWeave, Oracle and SoftBank, fell with it. Nvidia has invested in OpenAI, guaranteed data-center rent in Ohio and proposed a large customer-financing program, so a leadership change puts that support in question.
- AMD and Broadcom rose and European indexes set records as investors shifted away from the most exposed AI names.
- Beijing warned the US not to use the scandal to expand chip restrictions, a week before a planned Trump–Xi meeting. The Commerce Department is reported to be close to a rule limiting remote access to advanced chips in several more countries.
- A change in how Nvidia reports revenue by region removed the Singapore line that once made up over a fifth of sales. Senators want export records preserved.
- AI agents from the major labs are limited to paid plans because they are expensive to serve. Meta says its free agent needs a large efficiency gain to become profitable.
- Inference start-ups are buying their own GPUs and exploring loans because larger cloud providers favor end customers. Sequoia is backing an AI holding company that takes services businesses private.
- Republican strategists blame data-center opposition for midterm losses, making resistance to AI build-outs bipartisan. SEC enforcement against public companies fell to a 16-year low.

## Numbers Worth Remembering
- Nvidia: down more than 8% after hours and about 7% before Thursday's open; more than 13% lower over five sessions.
- Nvidia's support for OpenAI: a $30B investment and $105B of backstopped data-center rent.
- Nvidia's Singapore-billed revenue in the July 2025 quarter: $10.2B, 22% of the total.
- Estimated capital cost per user of Meta's free AI agent: $25–50 (Deutsche Bank).
- Recent AI holding-company buyouts: Baldwin $7.7B, ADT $15.3B, Amex GBT $6.3B.
- SEC actions against public companies in the past fiscal year: 16, a record low.
