## Top Takeaways
- CoreWeave's unsecured 2030 debt trades near a 15% yield while a bond secured on one of its leased sites yields about 8%. Credit markets are valuing the buildings above the tenant, so neocloud funding costs should keep climbing.
- Microsoft walked away from Nscale's 50MW UK site after grid power slipped to 2030 or later. With about 73GW of UK data center requests chasing a 45GW grid, power availability is now the bottleneck.
- Nvidia's sales to customers outside the big cloud providers reached about $40B, 45% of its data center business. That makes a neocloud shakeout a direct risk to Nvidia's shipments.
- Nvidia is pushing memory makers to build custom HBM base dies on TSMC's 3nm process, which shifts value toward TSMC and may narrow memory makers' pricing power (our inference).
- Anthropic may raise up to $100B at close to a $2T valuation, but a benefit trust appoints a majority of its board, so public investors get growth with limited control.
- China proposed export licensing for 23 AI technologies, which makes cross-border deals with Chinese AI firms harder.

## Numbers Worth Remembering
- US AI infrastructure spend: about 3.6% of GDP a year through 2032, versus about 2.2% for the 1800s railroads.
- 47 large US data center projects delayed, withdrawn or blocked in 2026.
- Data center bonds: over $100B this year, about a quarter of AI-related dollar issuance.
- H100 rental index: down about 3.8% on the week to roughly $2.81 an hour.
- China Q3 GDP +4.5%; Nasdaq 100 −5.5% the prior week.
