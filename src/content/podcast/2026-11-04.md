## Top Takeaways
- Democrats took both chambers of Congress. US futures recovered from an overnight slide, and yields, the dollar and crude moved lower on hopes that a split government means less policy upheaval.
- Candidates who campaigned on halting new data centers won several governor races, including Texas, and many local limits passed. That adds a permitting risk to AI construction plans.
- AMD's data-center unit roughly doubled its sales and now makes up most of the company's revenue. Its outlook topped forecasts, though an OpenAI warrant arrangement brought a sizable charge.
- Large investors are steering Anthropic toward a lower IPO valuation, around $1.5tn, ahead of a listing expected later this month.
- Apollo is lining up roughly $75bn of AI chips to lease to SpaceX and Anthropic, more than any specialist cloud provider, funded largely with insurance capital.
- US pressure is cutting Chinese customers out of American AI infrastructure. Oracle dropped a Tencent agreement, and Nvidia is limiting Chinese clients at partner clouds.
- Apple said rising memory prices will weigh on margins, a sign of the pricing power memory makers now have.
- China paused new IPO applications and added liquidity support after AI worries hit its stock market.
- GPU rental prices reached a three-month high, and current-generation DRAM kept edging up.
