## Top Takeaways
- September CPI rose to 3.7% a year, driven mostly by energy, with Brent near $110 on Middle East escalation. Hot inflation keeps the Fed leaning toward hikes ahead of its Oct 27–28 meeting.
- The Nasdaq 100 fell 5.5%, its worst week since April 2025, as failed data center IPOs (Firmus, AirTrunk) showed public buyers won't fund the build-out at current prices.
- CoreWeave's bonds fell into distressed territory, and Anthropic is paying about 10% on the riskiest slice of a $60B chip financing. Capital for AI infrastructure is getting expensive fast.
- Dell builds AI servers only after customers order, so its back-orders work as an early warning signal for AI demand.
- Memory stocks fell hardest on fears the price cycle has peaked, even as Dell says the shortage will tighten next year.

## Numbers Worth Remembering
- CPI: 3.7% year over year, up from 3.4%.
- Nasdaq 100: −5.5% on the week.
- 10-year Treasury: topped 5.3%, finished near 5.1%.
- CoreWeave: about −25% on the week; bonds below 80 cents on the dollar.
- Dell AI server back-orders: about $95B.
