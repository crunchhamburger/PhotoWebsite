## Top Takeaways
- Marvell agreed to acquire Lumentum, an optical components maker, in a stock-for-stock deal valued near $115bn, a bet that light-based links will connect the next generation of AI clusters. Closing is not expected before the first half of 2028.
- Nvidia is negotiating to guarantee more than $5bn of borrowing by Nscale, the cloud provider building an Anthropic campus in West Virginia. That would make Nvidia supplier, investor and credit backstop at once.
- Anthropic's quarterly revenue climbed to roughly $17bn, but costly short-term compute purchases pushed it to an operating loss shortly before its expected IPO.
- OpenAI reduced its fundraising goal by a third, to about $20bn.
- More AI spending is being financed through capital markets. Oracle announced large stock and bond sales, Amazon disclosed a big supplier financing facility, and S&P turned more cautious on Meta's credit, all while high-yield bonds sold off.
- The Nasdaq fell nearly 3% on Friday as Intel had its worst day in about half a century. Stocks recovered part of that on Monday.
- Crude dropped sharply on signs Washington prefers diplomacy with Iran, though Gulf officials warn of renewed conflict.
- GPU rental prices edged back up, and DDR5 memory kept rising, while a key NAND wafer price fell about 9% in a week.
